Viewer readiness check

For the supervising adult. This is a diagnostic aid, not a safety certification or a lens calibration tool.

1. Phone and browser

Motion not tested. Hold phone and gently turn it after granting permission.

2. Stereo comfort

Rotate to landscape. As an adult, inspect the two reference circles without a viewer first. If you use the viewer, stay seated and remove it immediately if uncomfortable or the image does not merge. The display is a generic alignment pattern, not viewer-specific distortion correction.

Tap twice after removing the viewer, or press Escape, to exit.
